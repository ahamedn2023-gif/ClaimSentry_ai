Human-in-the-loop claims triage

The agent investigates. A person decides.

A claimant uploads their paperwork. ClaimSentry fingerprints every file, examines it for signs of alteration, cross-checks it against what the claimant declared, scores the risk, and routes the file to the head of the right department. No claim is ever approved or rejected by the model — the department head makes that call, and every action lands in an audit trail.

Submit a claim Open the department console

1 · Intake

The claimant files the claim and attaches photos, invoices, medical reports or a repair estimate. Each file is hashed with SHA-256, stored privately, and never served back to anyone outside the owning department.

2 · Authenticity review

A multimodal model examines each document for altered regions, inconsistent fonts, arithmetic that does not add up, duplicated stamps, re-compression artifacts and contradictions with the declared claim. Deterministic rules run in parallel: duplicate submissions, future incident dates, delayed reporting, amount drift between the documents and the claim.

3 · Routing & decision

The file is scored, banded and routed — Auto, Health, Property, Life, Travel, or the Special Investigations Unit when the evidence warrants it. The department head sees the evidence, the agent's reasoning and the open questions, then approves, rejects, asks for more information or escalates.

What the agent looks for

  • Digitally altered or erased regions, inconsistent fonts, misaligned baselines
  • Cloned text blocks, pasted stamps and signatures, mirrored or cropped seals
  • Line items that do not sum, totals in words that disagree with digits
  • Dates that fall outside the policy period or before the incident
  • Screenshots of screenshots, recompression and circular JPEG artifacts
  • Untrimmed template placeholders and mismatched letterhead/footer
  • Wrong patient, wrong policy number, wrong currency, wrong service described
  • Photographs where the damage or the scene does not match the statement

Absence of evidence is not evidence of fraud. Poor scan quality alone never routes a claim to investigations — the routing rules require a concrete, citable indicator.

Built-in controls

  • Department heads must sign in. Queues and case files are authorized on the server against the signed-in identity and their assigned department.
  • Cross-department access is refused. A Health head asking for an Auto claim gets 403, not a redacted page.
  • State-changing requests are same-origin checked, so a claim cannot be decided from a foreign page.
  • Every step is logged — submission, storage, duplicate detection, agent assessment, routing, human decision — with the actor, timestamp and reasoning.

ClaimSentry runs on Cloudflare Workers with D1 and R2. Claimants must explicitly opt in before any claim text or document is sent to Gemini. Without consent or a configured GEMINI_API_KEY, assessment stays rules-only.
